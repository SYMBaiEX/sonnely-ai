## Inspect usage

Open Usage and choose a UTC date range. In **Request breakdown**, choose **By model** or **By key**.

<div className="docs-guide-facts">

- **Spend** shows what settled requests charged. Use the displayed precision when comparing fractions of a cent.
- **Tokens** shows the volume billed over the period.
- **Requests** counts activity for the selected period.

</div>

For the exact charge for one request, open **Recent requests** and find its row. **Copy request reference** lets you match that row to the `X-Sonnely-Request-Id` response header. Recent requests shows the exact amount, including fractions of a cent.

- **Charged.** The request settled and the amount is final.
- **Reserved or Under review.** Credits are still held. Check the same reference before retrying.
- **Released.** The request failed and its reservation was released.

## Export to UsageMax

Export up to 100 settled inference requests from the selected period. A batch contains model, timestamps, token counts, and ledger charges.

- **Download a batch** to import it yourself.
- **Send to a collector** with a write-only UsageMax key. Reuse the installation UUID for deduplication and store collector credentials in UsageMax or your secret manager.

[UsageMax collector documentation](https://usagemax.com/docs)

## Platform reconciliation

Authorized Sonnely operators can inspect request spans, inference attempts, evidence checks, holds and final settlements in Admin. Per-request cost lookups run on a schedule. Finalized invoice exports can be imported separately and matched against billing references. Reconciliation can revise the compute cost record without changing the customer charge. Treasury payout approval and wallet signing are manual.

Alerts can be delivered through the configured operations inbox. Admin reports delivery status, unmatched fees, open holds, and truncated report coverage.
