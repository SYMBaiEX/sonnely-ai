## Wallet and profile

Connect a Solana wallet in the console and approve the sign-in message. Signing in uses a wallet signature. Sending USDC is a separate approval in Billing. Use the same wallet to return to your credits, keys, and requests.

In [Settings](/console/settings), set a display name and an optional username. Usernames use 3–24 letters, numbers, or underscores and start with a letter. Sonnely normalizes usernames to lowercase. Your username can appear in referral activity.

## Invite a user

Copy your personal invite link from [Referrals](/console/referrals). The pipeline shows attributed sign-ups, mainnet funding, and paid inference. Use these activity states to track adoption.

## Browser and account data

Optional playground history is stored locally in your browser. Manage or clear it in Settings. Your wallet-scoped usage and credit ledger remain available across devices when you sign in with the same wallet.
