## Fund directly with USDC

1. **Choose an amount.** In [Billing](/console/billing), enter any positive USDC amount up to six decimal places.
2. **Review the transfer.** Check the amount, recipient, and network. Your wallet pays Solana transaction fees in SOL and may need to fund token-account creation.
3. **Approve payment.** Pay with the connected wallet, use the Solana Pay link, or scan its QR code on another device.
4. **Check your balance.** Credits appear once the transaction reaches finality.

> **Returning to a payment?** Use **Resume payment** or check the transaction signature if you closed the browser before confirmation.

On Solana Seeker, open Sonnely in Android Chrome and connect with **Solana mobile wallet**. Select the account you intend to use before continuing to payment.

## Understand reservations

- **Reservation.** Sonnely holds the estimated maximum before sending a completion.
- **Settlement.** Sonnely records the actual charge and releases the remaining hold.

For inference rates, see [provider pricing](/docs/providers#compare-prices). Contact [support](/contact) with the transaction signature or billing reference if a payment or reservation stays open.
