## Fund directly with USDC

1. In [Billing](/console/billing), enter any positive USDC amount up to six decimal places.
2. Review the amount, recipient, and network. Your wallet pays Solana transaction fees in SOL and may need to fund token-account creation.
3. Pay with the connected wallet, use the Solana Pay link, or scan its QR code on another device.
4. Credits post after finality and appear in the balance. Use **Resume payment** or check the transaction signature if you closed the browser before confirmation.

On Solana Seeker, open Sonnely in Android Chrome and connect with **Solana mobile wallet**. Select the account you intend to use before continuing to payment.

## Fund with other crypto

When available in Billing, **Fund with other crypto** quotes a NEAR Intents swap of a supported asset into USDC in your connected Solana wallet. Review the deposit instructions, amount, network, minimum output, and refund wallet. After USDC arrives, complete normal Sonnely checkout. The swap by itself does not add Sonnely credits.

## Understand reservations

Sonnely reserves the estimated maximum before a completion or compute rental. Final settlement records the actual charge and releases the remaining hold. For inference rates, see [provider pricing](/docs/providers#compare-prices). For compute charges, see [Blackboxes](/docs/blackboxes#charges). Contact [support](/contact) with the transaction signature or billing reference if a payment or reservation stays open.
