## Fund directly with USDC

1. In [Billing](/console/billing), enter any positive USDC amount up to six decimal places.
2. Review the amount, recipient, and network. Your wallet pays Solana transaction fees in SOL and may need to fund token-account creation.
3. Pay with the connected wallet, use the Solana Pay link, or scan its QR code on another device.
4. Credits post after finality and appear in the balance. Use **Resume payment** or check the transaction signature if you closed the browser before confirmation.

On Solana Seeker, open Sonnely in Android Chrome and connect with **Solana mobile wallet**. Select the account you intend to use before continuing to payment.

## Fund with other crypto

Use **Fund with other crypto** to swap a supported asset into USDC on Solana. Select your connected Solana wallet as the recipient in NEAR Intents. Review the deposit instructions, network, amount, and refund wallet before sending. Once the USDC arrives in that wallet, use **Buy credits** in Sonnely.

## Understand reservations

Sonnely reserves the estimated maximum before a completion. Final settlement records the actual charge and releases the remaining hold. For inference rates, see [provider pricing](/docs/providers#compare-prices). Contact [support](/contact) with the transaction signature or billing reference if a payment or reservation stays open.
