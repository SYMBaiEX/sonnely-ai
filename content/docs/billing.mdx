## Fund directly with USDC

Use the Solana wallet you want tied to your workspace. The header shows the active wallet and credit balance.

<div className="docs-guide-procedure">

1. **Choose an amount.** Enter a positive amount in USDC in Billing. You can use up to six decimal places.
2. **Review the transfer.** Check the amount, recipient, and network. Your wallet pays Solana transaction fees in SOL and may need to fund token-account creation.
3. **Approve payment.** Pay with the connected wallet, use the Solana Pay link, or scan its QR code on another device.
4. **Check your balance.** Credits appear once the transaction reaches finality.

</div>

> **Returning to a payment?** Use **Resume payment** or check the transaction signature if you closed the browser before confirmation. If you already sent a transfer using the link or QR code, wait for finality in Billing before approving another transfer.

Archive a payment request from history when you no longer plan to use it. You can still check its status, and a qualifying transfer that was already sent can still be credited.

On Solana Seeker, open Sonnely in Android Chrome and connect with **Solana mobile wallet**. Select the account you intend to use before continuing to payment.

Paying from the connected wallet requires support for Solana v1 or v0 transactions. You can also open the Solana Pay request in a compatible wallet.

## Fund with SOL

In **Buy credits**, select **SOL** and enter the USD amount of credits to buy. Review your quoted SOL amount and expiration, then approve the direct treasury transfer in your wallet or scan its Solana Pay QR code. The quote uses a recent SOL/USD price and lasts five minutes. Credits post after a qualifying payment finalizes. If you sent SOL after the quote expired, contact support with the transaction signature.

## Fund with Confidential USDC

1. Select **Confidential USDC** in Billing and enter the USDC amount you want the treasury to receive.
2. In Solflare, select the wallet you used to sign in, then open **Private Mode → Send**. Copy the recipient address and amount from Billing. Confirm Solflare labels the transfer **Private** and review the wallet fees before sending.
3. Copy the transaction signature from Solflare’s activity and paste it into Billing. Select **Verify payment**.
4. Track the payment in **Payment history**. Some payments require treasury review to confirm the encrypted amount and asset. Credits appear after verification.

Solflare requires a funded Private Mode balance and SOL for fees. The transfer hides the asset and amount on-chain. The sender and recipient addresses remain visible. If you sent a payment before the request expired or was cancelled, open it in **Payment history** to submit the signature. [Solflare Private Mode guide](https://www.solflare.com/guides/how-to-use-private-mode/)

## Understand reservations

<div className="docs-guide-facts">

- **Reservation.** Sonnely holds the estimated maximum before sending a completion.
- **Settlement.** Sonnely records the actual charge and releases the remaining hold.

</div>

Check the balance in Billing and the charge for an inference request in [Recent requests](/console/usage#recent-request-references). If a payment or reservation stays open, share its transaction signature or request reference with [support](/contact).
