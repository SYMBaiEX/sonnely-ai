---
title: API keys and budgets
description: Create, budget, rotate, and revoke keys from the console.
---

## Create a key

In [API keys](/console/keys), give each app or deployment its own named key so you can identify its activity in Usage. Store it in a server-side secret store when it appears. Use the key as the bearer token for completion requests.

## Set spend limits

- **Daily budget.** The limit resets at midnight UTC.
- **Lifetime budget.** This caps spending over the life of the key.
- **Expiry.** Set an end date for temporary keys.

Sonnely checks your balance and the key’s budgets against the maximum reservation before forwarding a request. Unused credit returns at settlement.

Use `max_cost_microusd` for a per-request ceiling. A limit of `1000` permits a reservation of up to $0.001. Lower the output allowance or choose a route with lower rates if the reservation exceeds your cap.

## Rotate or revoke

Create a replacement, update your server-side configuration, and send a test request. Revoke the previous key after verifying the replacement.

> **Compromised key?** Revoke it immediately and review [Usage](/console/usage) by key.
