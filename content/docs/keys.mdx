## Create a key

In API keys, choose **Create key** and give each app or deployment its own name. You can find the name in **Request breakdown → By key** in [Usage](/console/usage#request-breakdown).

> **The full key appears once.** Copy it into your server’s secret store. Completion requests use it as the bearer token.

## Set spend limits

<div className="docs-guide-facts">

- **Daily cap (USD).** Resets at midnight UTC.
- **Lifetime cap (USD).** Caps cumulative spend on the key.
- **Expires (UTC).** The key expires at the end of the selected date.

</div>

Sonnely checks your balance and the key’s budgets against the maximum reservation before forwarding a request. Unused credit returns at settlement.

Use `max_cost_microusd` for a per-request ceiling. A limit of `1000` permits a reservation of up to $0.001. Lower the output allowance or choose a route with lower rates if the reservation exceeds your cap.

## Rotate or revoke

<div className="docs-guide-procedure">

1. **Create a replacement.** Copy the new key into your server’s configuration.
2. **Test it.** Send a request and confirm it appears under the new key in Usage.
3. **Revoke the old key.** Clients still using it will lose API access immediately.

</div>

> **Compromised key?** Revoke it immediately and review [Usage](/console/usage) by key.
