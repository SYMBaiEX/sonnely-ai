# App

The Sonnely application: the public site, the documentation, the console a
workspace is run from, and the inference API, all served from `apps/app`.

- [Navigation](./nav.md): where the visitor is and where they can go.
- [Session](./session.md): a Solana wallet's claim on a workspace.
- [Site](./site/): the public pages and what is on them.
- [Models](./models.md): the model catalog and its pricing.
- [Docs](./docs/): the guides and reference the product ships with.
- [Console](./console/): the workspace surface: playground, keys, usage,
  billing, teams and admin.
- [Keys](./keys.md): API keys, their limits and their lifetime.
- [Usage](./usage.md): metering, request logs and analytics.
- [Billing](./billing/): the credit ledger, Solana funding and confidential
  payments.
- [Teams](./teams.md): workspaces shared with a team.
- [Support](./support.md): support tickets and referrals.
- [Admin](./admin/): the operator surface.
- [Gateway](./gateway/): the OpenAI-compatible inference API and its receipts.
